Waymo's reported nationwide robotaxi fleet expanded from **about 700 vehicles in August 2024** to **more than 4,000 in September 2026**. These are dated reports of the fleet size, **not** measurements of the number of cars driving simultaneously.

The chart joins individual observations to make the growth easier to see. **Straight connecting lines are visual guides**, not estimates of monthly fleet counts. Values reported as “more than” are plotted at their stated lower bounds; “about” denotes an approximate number. Since these are different reporting snapshots and estimates, the numbers should not be treated as a complete time series.

## Interpretation

A commercial fleet includes vehicles that may be charging, maintained, parked, or otherwise off duty. Fleet figures do not measure cars actively carrying passengers at any one time. Separate regulatory recall totals also exist, but affected vehicle populations vary by recall and are not directly comparable to this series.
