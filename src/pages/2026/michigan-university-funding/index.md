These charts cover Michigan's **15 public universities**, using selected fiscal years (not a continuous annual series). Dollar amounts are in **billions**.

The first chart shows **nominal revenue**, the second the **national Consumer Price Index** (1979 = 100), and the third revenue adjusted to **2025 dollars**. Select the tabs to switch views.

**Important:** Figures for **1979–1997 are approximate reconstructions** from published state appropriations and rounded funding shares; later amounts are sourced from legislative fiscal-agency general fund revenue tables. The charts should not be interpreted as a per-student comparison. Inflation adjustment uses annual-average national CPI-U, not a university-specific cost index.

**Sources:** [Michigan House Fiscal Agency higher education reports](https://www.house.mi.gov/hfa/), [Michigan Senate Fiscal Agency higher education reports](https://www.senate.michigan.gov/sfa/), and [Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis historical inflation data](https://www.minneapolisfed.org/about-us/monetary-policy/inflation-calculator/consumer-price-index-1913-). The 2025 funding amounts are from the House Fiscal Agency general fund revenue breakdown. Calculated historical estimates and rounded fiscal-year amounts can be inspected in [the accompanying JSON data](./data.json).

**Interpretation:** Michigan's general fund revenue grew even after inflation adjustment, but real state appropriations declined relative to their 1979 level. These figures do not measure all university revenues or changes in student enrollment.
