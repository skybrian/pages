These are **forest-use notifications, not confirmed completed clear-cuts**.
The year refers to the notification data; the graph should not be read as the
area actually cut that year.

Source: [Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke), “Area treated with fellings (from notifications of forest use)”](https://statdb.luke.fi/PxWeb/pxweb/en/LUKE/LUKE__met__methoi/0400_methoi.px/),
using **Area (hectares)**, **WHOLE COUNTRY**, **TOTAL (forest ownership category)**,
and **Clearcutting**, for all available years (2015–2025).
The underlying data provider is the Finnish Forest Centre.

Hectares are divided by 100 to obtain square kilometres (km²).
The table was updated June 24, 2026; this snapshot was retrieved October 31, 2026.
Luke notes that the 2020–2023 figures were corrected on October 2, 2024.
