Source: [Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke), “Area treated with fellings (from notifications of forest use)”](https://statdb.luke.fi/PxWeb/pxweb/en/LUKE/LUKE__met__methoi/0400_methoi.px/).

Notifications are normally submitted to the Finnish Forest Centre at least 10 days before harvesting, and no more than three years beforehand. This graph totals the areas from clear-cutting notifications by submission year. The notifications do not confirm that the clear-cutting took place.

For hectares, multiply by 100.
