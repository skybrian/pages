Crime includes homicide, rape, aggravated assault, robbery, and electronic theft.

Sources: [BART quarterly performance reports](https://www.bart.gov/about/reports) and [monthly police reports](https://www.bart.gov/about/police/reports) for crimes, and [monthly ridership workbooks](https://www.bart.gov/about/reports/ridership) for trips.

From July 2019 onward, crime counts come from quarterly reports. Earlier quarters are reconstructed from monthly reports and year-to-date totals. Some counts are estimated from chart bars; detailed methods and caveats are in the JSON.

In January 2025, reporting changed from one offense per incident to multiple offenses if more than one crime happened per incident, so the numbers aren't directly comparable.
