The Census income brackets are aggregated into under $35,000, $35,000–$99,999, and $100,000 or more, in inflation-adjusted 2024 dollars.

Shares are each band’s count divided by the reported total households for that year. Values in the source table are rounded, so the bands may not sum exactly to the reported total.

**Source:** [U.S. Census Bureau, Historical Income Tables: Households, Table H-17](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/time-series/demo/income-poverty/historical-income-households.html).
