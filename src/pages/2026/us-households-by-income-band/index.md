U.S. household counts and shares by income band from 1967 to 2024, in 2024 dollars. The Census brackets are aggregated into under $35,000, $35,000–$99,999, and $100,000 or more.

The income-band household counts are shown in millions. Shares are each band’s count divided by the reported total households for that year. Values in the source table are rounded, so the bands may not sum exactly to the reported total.

**Source:** [U.S. Census Bureau, Historical Income Tables: Households, Table H-17](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/time-series/demo/income-poverty/historical-income-households.html).
